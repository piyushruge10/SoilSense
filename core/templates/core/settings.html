Arduino UNO hardware connections

Example wiring for a capacitive soil-moisture sensor, DHT22 air sensor, and DS18B20 soil-temperature probe.

SensorSensor pinArduino UNO pinPurpose / notes
Capacitive moisture sensorVCC5VUse the sensor's rated supply voltage.
Capacitive moisture sensorGNDGNDShare ground with the other sensors.
Capacitive moisture sensorAO / AOUTA0Analog moisture reading; calibrate dry and wet values in your sketch.
DHT22 humidity sensorVCC / +5V*Reads air humidity and air temperature.
DHT22 humidity sensorGND / -GNDPlace above soil; it measures air, not soil moisture.
DHT22 humidity sensorDATA / OUTD2Bare sensors need a 4.7–10 kΩ pull-up from DATA to VCC; many modules include one.
DS18B20 temperature probeVDD / red5VProbe for soil/root-zone temperature.
DS18B20 temperature probeGND / blackGNDWire colors may differ; confirm your probe's datasheet.
DS18B20 temperature probeDATA / yellowD3Add a 4.7 kΩ pull-up resistor between DATA and 5V.

Before powering up: connect all grounds, verify the pin labels and voltage rating printed on your modules, and never connect 5V to an Arduino input pin. Use either sensor setup that matches your sketch; the DHT22 reports air temperature, while the DS18B20 probe reports soil temperature. For a bare 4-pin DHT sensor, check its datasheet for pin order. Calibrate the moisture sensor's analog readings for your soil, then have the sketch send temperature, humidity, and moisture as one CSV or JSON line at the configured baud rate.
